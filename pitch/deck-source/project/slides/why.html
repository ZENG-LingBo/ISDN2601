Why plans fall through

No one is in charge

In a group, everyone thinks someone else will act (Darley & Latané, 1968).

No set time or place

“Soon” is not a plan. Plans with a set time and place are much more likely to happen (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006).

Fear of looking too eager

We underestimate how much friends like us and want to hear from us (Boothby et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2023).

It’s always dinner

Friends who can’t eat spicy food or are saving money say “whatever”. A group can end up doing what nobody wants (Harvey, 1974).
