Why plans fall through

No one owns the plan

In a group chat, everyone thinks someone else will organise it (Darley & Latané, 1968).

No time, no place

“Soon” is not a plan. Plans with a set time and place are much more likely to happen (Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006).

Fear of looking too eager

We underestimate how much friends like us and want to hear from us (Boothby et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2023).

1:05–1:25 Why do plans fall through? No one owns the plan: everyone thinks someone else will organise it. There’s no time and no place: “soon” is not a plan. And we’re afraid of looking too eager, but friends want to hear from us more than we think.
