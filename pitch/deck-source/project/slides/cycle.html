We want to meet, but feel relieved when plans fall through

We say yes

because saying no feels rude. 77% have done this (Givi & Kirk, 2023).

We regret it

Closer to the day, 56% of US under-30s often don’t want to go (YouGov, 2022).

We feel relieved

1 in 3

often feel relieved when a friend cancels (YouGov, 2019).

Then guilty

86% feel guilty about cancelling (IPA, 2025).
