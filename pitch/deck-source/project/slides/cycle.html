We want to meet, but feel relieved when plans fall through

We say yes

because saying no feels rude. In one study, 77% had said yes to plans they didn’t want (Givi & Kirk, 2023).

We regret it

As the day gets closer, 56% of US adults under 30 often don’t want to go (YouGov, 2022).

We feel relieved

1 in 3

often feel relieved when a friend cancels (YouGov, 2019).

We feel guilty

86% feel guilty about cancelling (IPA, 2025).

0:50–1:05 Here’s the strange part. We say yes because saying no feels rude. As the day gets closer, we wish we had said no. When a friend cancels, one in three of us feels relieved… and then guilty.
