I compared the ideas on five criteria: desirability for Mandy, feasibility for a student team, viability, fit with 2030, and privacy. I also counted how many pain points each one solves. No idea wins on everything. The chat assistant solves the most, but a student team can't build it and it reads the chat. Minibus is the most realistic, but it misses the new insight. The ritual is cheap and private, but it doesn't fit split timetables. So I didn't pick one idea as it is. I combined the strongest parts.
