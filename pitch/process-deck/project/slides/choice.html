The direction I'm building is Minibus 2030. It keeps what already works: private answers and automatic confirmation. Then it adds three things for the new insight. Each friend sets food and budget limits in private. Each plan starts with a mood and a budget instead of a restaurant. And Minibus suggests 3 things everyone can do, so nobody has to speak up. From idea 4, free times can be filled in from a timetable. From idea 2, there's a mock-up of Minibus as an assistant inside the group chat, to show where it could go by 2030. This is a concept. It isn't finished, and no real group has tried it yet.
