Research: what I searched for

5 questions

  1. How lonely are young people in Hong Kong?
  2. How often do plans with friends fall through?
  3. How do people feel when a plan is cancelled?
  4. Why does planning in a group fail?
  5. How do groups choose what to do? (added later)

Where I looked

  • Hong Kong surveys: HKFYG, Hong Kong Family Welfare Society
  • Government reports: US Surgeon General
  • Psychology journals, for the reasons behind the numbers
  • Polling firms: YouGov, Opinium

How I checked it

  • Each figure checked on the publisher’s own page, or in news reports of it
  • Sample size and method noted for every poll
  • Surveys by app companies kept off the slides
