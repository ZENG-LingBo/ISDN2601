Idea 3 of 6: a standing ritual

The group keeps one fixed time each week, like “Thursday dinner club”, and meets by default.

How it works for Mandy

  1. At the start of term, the group picks one evening, once.
  2. Every week it happens unless someone calls it off. Nobody has to ask.
  3. Whoever can come, comes.
