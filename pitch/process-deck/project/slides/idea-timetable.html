Idea 4 of 6: a shared timetable matcher

Friends connect their university timetables, and it shows when everyone is free.

How it works for Mandy

  1. Mandy and her friends each add their timetable once.
  2. It shows that all 5 are free on Friday after 4pm.
  3. Someone still has to choose that time, and what to do.

Good

Nobody types in their free times. It solves the clash that’s hardest to sort out by hand.

Bad

Jobs and commutes aren’t in a university timetable. Sharing your timetable shows a lot about you.

In 2030

Calendars could share only when you’re free, not what you’re doing.

The fourth idea is the strongest on one pain point: timetables. Nobody has to type anything. But it only finds a time. Someone still has to pick it and decide what to do, so the asking problem is only partly solved. It also misses part-time jobs and commutes, and sharing a whole timetable is a lot to ask.
