Research: the limits of this evidence

What the evidence can’t tell me

  • The numbers on cancelled plans are from the UK and the US. I found no Hong Kong survey on this.
  • Most polls are online panels, not random samples.
  • A few sources were checked through news reports and search results, not the full paper.
  • I haven’t interviewed any HKUST students yet.

What I did about it

  • Every number on the slides says where it’s from: Hong Kong, the UK or the US.
  • Two surveys run by app companies (Yubo and Sky Mobile) stayed off the slides.
  • The persona says it’s based on the research, not on a real person.
  • Interviews with HKUST students are the next step.
