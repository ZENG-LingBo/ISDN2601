These are the findings I relied on. The first two are from Hong Kong and show that loneliness is common, including among students. The rest show the pattern behind it: people agree to plans, regret it, feel relieved when plans are cancelled, and then feel guilty. The last column shows who was asked, because that decides how far each number can be trusted.
