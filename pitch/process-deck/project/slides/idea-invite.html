Idea 5 of 6: a low-pressure open invite

Anyone posts “I’m free Friday 7pm, anyone?”, and whoever wants to can join.

How it works for Mandy

  1. Mandy posts a time and something she can do, like a walk and noodles that aren’t spicy.
  2. Friends tap “Join”. Nobody has to say no.
  3. If 2 people join, it goes ahead.

Good

Saying no costs nothing. The person who posts picks something they can do.

Bad

If nobody joins, the person who posted feels worse. Easy to join, and just as easy to drop out.

In 2030

Friends could see each other’s open invites on their phone’s home screen.

The fifth idea takes the pressure off asking. You don't invite anyone directly. You say you're free, and people join if they want. Mandy can pick something she can eat. But the person who posts still takes a risk: if nobody joins, it feels worse than not asking. And joining with one tap is as easy to undo as it is to do.
