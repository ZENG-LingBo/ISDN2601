Next steps: interviews, then a class test

1. Interviews

15 minutes each with 5 to 8 HKUST students. For example:

  • Is there a plan stuck in one of your group chats?
  • When your group picks what to do, do you ever stay quiet about food or money?

2. A class test

4 to 6 friend groups for one week. Half use Minibus, and half use a WhatsApp poll. Then compare who answered, which plans got a time, and which groups met.

3. Before the test

  • Written approval from the instructor
  • A signed consent form from everyone
  • A date to delete all the data
