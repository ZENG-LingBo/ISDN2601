This is the How Might We question, checked against the lecturers' rules. The one change was the ending: the draft said “instead of fading”, which is negative, so it now says “keep their friendships close”. After the new insight, I checked the question again. I kept it, because a meetup that half the group can't enjoy isn't a real meetup. For ideas, I also wrote a narrower question: how might we help university friend groups choose something everyone can enjoy, in order to include every friend without anyone having to speak up?
