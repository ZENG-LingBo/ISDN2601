The brief: leisure in 2030

The theme asks how free time can move from killing time to meaningful downtime, with more live, in-person experiences.

Why friend groups

Free time with friends is the kind of leisure the brief describes. But many students spend that time alone on their phones, and 83.7% of young people in Hong Kong report moderate to high loneliness (HKFYG, 2026).

Why this problem

Almost every student knows the group chat where everyone agrees to meet and nobody does. I can study it on campus, with people I can reach this term.

Why not other groups

Classmates already chose remote workers, gamers, gym goers, pet owners, urban families, walkers, the young elderly and short waiting times. University friend groups didn’t overlap with any of them.
