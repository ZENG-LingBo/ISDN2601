Idea 6 of 6: campus or venue passes

The university or local venues offer cheap group passes that expire, which gives the group a reason and a deadline.

How it works for Mandy

  1. Mandy’s group gets a pass for 4 to 6 people, like bowling or a climbing wall.
  2. It ends in 2 weeks, so the group has to pick a date.
  3. It’s already paid for, so cancelling feels like a waste.
