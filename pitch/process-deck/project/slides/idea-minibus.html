Idea 1 of 6: Minibus, a group planner

Friends answer in private, and the plan is confirmed once enough people can make the same time.

How it works for Mandy

  1. She starts a plan and pastes the link into the group chat.
  2. Friends tap the times they’re free. Names aren’t shown next to answers until the plan is confirmed.
  3. When enough people can make one time, Minibus confirms it and writes the message for the chat.

The first idea is the prototype I've already built. It's named after Hong Kong's red minibuses, which leave once enough seats are filled. A plan works the same way. It solves the first two pain points well. It only partly helps with timetables, because people still tap their times by hand, and with relief and guilt, because it asks you to change your times instead of cancelling. It does nothing for the new insight: the group still has to choose what to do.
