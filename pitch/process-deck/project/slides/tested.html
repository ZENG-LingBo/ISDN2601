Testing

The current Minibus went through 19 review rounds covering design, code, usability, accessibility, privacy and security. Each round found problems, and every serious one was fixed. The biggest lesson was about privacy: hiding names isn't enough, because friends can sometimes work out answers from the totals. The app now says so, and whether to hide totals is a question for real users. To be clear, the reviewers were AI agents. No real group has used it yet, so that's the next step.
