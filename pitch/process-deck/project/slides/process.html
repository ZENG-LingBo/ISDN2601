The process in 9 steps

1

Brief

Read the theme and chose a problem to work on.

2

Research

Found evidence on loneliness and cancelled plans, and noted its limits.

3

User

Narrowed the evidence to one target user and a persona, Mandy.

4

Pain points

Listed Mandy’s pain points and built an empathy map.

5

Problem

Wrote and rewrote the problem statement, then the How Might We question.

6

New insight

Added a second problem: choosing what to do stalls.

7

Ideas

Came up with 6 different ways to solve it.

8

Choice

Compared the ideas against 5 criteria and chose one direction.

9

Testing

Reviewed the prototype in 19 rounds, and planned tests with real students.
