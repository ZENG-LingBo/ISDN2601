Pain points: without school, a game needs 3 things

1

A time when everyone’s free

At school

The timetable did it.

Now

8 friends have 8 timetables, part-time jobs and long commutes.

2

A court

At school

The school court.

Now

Bots and resellers compete for public courts. Bot accounts now get a 360-day ban (LCSD, 2025).

3

The nerve to be the weakest player

At school

A team at your level.

Now

People picked last at school report less activity later in life (Cardinal et al., 2013).

0:50–1:03. Count the three things on your fingers. Without school, a game needs three things: a time when everyone’s free, a court she can book, and the nerve to be the weakest player.
