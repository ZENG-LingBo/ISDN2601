Why: without school, a game needs 3 things

1

A group that’s free at the same time

At school

The timetable put everyone in the same place.

Now

8 friends have 8 timetables, part-time jobs and long commutes.

2

A court

At school

The school court was always there.

Now

Bots and resellers book public courts first. Accounts that use bots now get a 360-day ban (LCSD, 2025).

3

The nerve to be the weakest player

At school

A coach and a team at your level.

Now

Students who were picked last at school report less activity later in life (Cardinal et al., 2013).

0:39–0:58. Count the three things on your fingers. Without school, a game needs three things: a group that’s free at the same time, a court that bots haven’t booked, and the nerve to be the weakest player. People picked last at school say they’re less active as adults.
