Target user: Mandy, Year 2 at HKUST

Hong Kong university students who played sport at school and want to play with friends again.

M

Mandy, 20

A persona based on the research

Played on her school basketball team. Now she and 7 friends from her hall have different timetables. Most nights she plays games on her phone.

“I miss playing. It’s just hard to get a game going.”

Pain points

8 people are never free at the same time.

Public courts are booked before she can get one.

She’s out of practice and worries she’ll hold the team back.

In a big group, some people sit out and watch.

Her phone is easier, so she spends the evening on it.
