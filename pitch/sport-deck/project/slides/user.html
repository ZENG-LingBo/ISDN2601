Target user and empathy map: Mandy, Year 2 at HKUST

Mandy, 20

A persona based on the research

A university student who played sport at school. She and 7 friends from her hall all have different timetables.

“I miss playing. It’s just hard to get a game going.”

Says

  • “We should play sometime.”
  • “I’m so out of shape.”

Thinks

  • “I don’t want to hold the team back.”
  • “Who’s even free on Friday?”

Does

  • Looks for a court, finds none free
  • Plays games on her phone alone

Feels

  • Misses the team and the fun
  • Nervous about playing badly in front of friends
0:15–0:30. Point to “Says” when you quote her. Mandy is my target user. She misses the team, but she worries she’ll play badly in front of friends. So she says, “We should play sometime,” and opens her phone.
