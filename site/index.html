ISDN 1001 project

Demos for an HKUST design project on leisure in 2030.

Freeze

A camera game for everyone in front of one laptop. A shape appears on the screen. Fill it with your bodies, then hold still.

Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop, and allow the camera when your browser asks. No camera? You can play with practice players.

Play Freeze

Minibus

The prototype for the project's first topic, which the professor turned down. Friends say if they're in and when they're free, and the plan is confirmed once enough people can make the same time.

This demo uses pretend friends on one device.

Open the Minibus demo

What you type stays on your device. In the Minibus demo, a message or plan goes to WhatsApp or Google Calendar only if you choose to send it there. In Freeze, the camera picture stays on your laptop and isn't recorded.
