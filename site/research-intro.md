## Where the project is now

- **Topic.** The first topic, friend groups whose plans never happen, was turned down. The new topic is why university students stop playing sport and games together after secondary school.
- **Problem statement (draft).** University students want to play together, but a game needs a time when everyone's free, a court and the nerve to be the weakest player. So they spend their free time on their phones.
- **How Might We (draft).** How might we make starting an in-person game as easy as reaching for a phone, for university friend groups, in order to help them move and laugh together more often?
- **Prototype for the next stage.** Freeze: party games for everyone in front of one laptop. An AI model on the laptop finds each player's body, counts the players and keeps score. There's nothing to buy, nothing to book, no running and no watchers. [Try Freeze](/freeze/)
- **Slides.** The current pitch, the design process for the first topic, and the first topic's pitch: [all slides](/slides/)
- **What's missing.** The persona and empathy map still come from published research, not from real students. The plan below fixes that.
