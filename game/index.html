Freeze

Party games for everyone in front of one laptop. The laptop sees your bodies, counts the players and keeps score.

  1. Stand back. Freeze finds up to 6 players and gives each one a name tag.
  2. Pick a game. Fill a shape with your bodies, show the right number of hands, or copy a pose.
  3. Do it, then freeze. You get points for getting it right and for holding still.

Freeze does its body tracking on this laptop. The video isn't recorded or sent anywhere. The first time you start the camera, Freeze downloads its body-tracking code from jsDelivr and its model from Google, about 6 MB.
