Freeze

Fill the shape on the screen with your bodies, then hold still. Everyone the camera can see is on the team.

How many of you are playing?

  1. Learn the room. Everyone steps out of the picture for a few seconds.
  2. Fill the shape. You have 10 seconds to get into it together.
  3. Freeze. Hold still for 3 seconds. You get points for filling the shape and for holding still.

Freeze runs on this laptop. The video isn't recorded or sent anywhere.

Set up the laptop

  • Put the laptop on a table.
  • Stand about 3 metres from the laptop.
  • Check that the camera can see all of you, at least from the waist up.
  • When you choose Learn the room, everyone has 5 seconds to step out of the picture, so Freeze can learn what the empty room looks like.

Now step back in. You should turn teal. If parts of the empty room turn teal too, learn the room again.
